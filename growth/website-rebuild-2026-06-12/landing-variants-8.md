# Muffin & Co — 8 landing page variants

Positioning: "AI Back Office, Managed" for micro-SMBs. CTA: 3 founding client slots → 20-min workflow call. Pricing shown: from £200/mo + setup. Brand voice per brand-spec.md: premium, calm, operational, specific. No fake testimonials — all claims must be true today.

---

## V1 — The Tool Gap

**Hook:** "You're already paying for AI. It still isn't doing your admin."
**Subhead:** Subscriptions don't run a business. Wiring does. We connect the AI you already pay for to the work you actually do — invoices, bookings, follow-ups — and keep it running.

**Structure:** Hero → the gap (tools vs plumbing: "ChatGPT can't see your spreadsheet. Claude doesn't know your accountant.") → what wired-in looks like (3 concrete flows) → pricing → founding slots CTA.

**CTA framing:** "We're taking 3 founding clients. 20 minutes, we map one workflow, you decide."

---

## V2 — The Assistant You Can't Afford

**Hook:** "The assistant you can't afford costs £200 a month."
**Subhead:** A part-time admin is £900+/month and still needs managing. An AI back office does the same repetitive work for less than your software bill — set up and run for you.

**Structure:** Hero → cost ledger (part-time admin vs unused subscriptions vs us, in a table) → what the assistant does all day → how "managed" works → CTA.

**CTA framing:** Same founding-slot offer, anchored against hiring.

## V3 — Run Your Business

**Hook:** "Run your business. We'll run the back office."
**Subhead:** Muffin & Co builds and manages the admin engine behind your business — quotes, invoices, follow-ups, reconciliation prep — so your evenings are yours again.

**Structure:** Classic: hero → 3 benefit panels → 3-step process (Map / Build / Run) → pricing → CTA.

**CTA framing:** "Book a 20-minute workflow call."

## V4 — Midnight Invoicing

**Hook:** "It's 11pm and you're doing invoices again."
**Subhead:** You didn't start a business to spend nights in spreadsheets. We turn your weekly admin grind into a system that runs while you sleep — built, monitored and fixed by us.

## V5 — One Person + AI

**Hook:** "One person + AI is a real business model. If someone sets it up."
**Subhead:** The leanest businesses in the world now run on a founder and a stack of AI tools. The catch: someone has to wire the stack. That's us.

**Structure:** Trend → the setup gap → our role (your AI operations partner) → what a wired stack looks like → pricing → CTA.

**CTA framing:** "Become one of our 3 founding one-person giants."

## V6 — The Spreadsheet Is the Business

**Hook:** "Your whole business lives in a spreadsheet. We make it run itself."
**Subhead:** Jobs in. Invoices out. Accounts up to date. We take the spreadsheet you already run your business on and turn it into a back office that does its own admin.

**Structure:** Hero → anatomy of a small-business back office (spreadsheet → invoices → accounting, drawn as a ledger diagram) → 3 named example flows (job sheet → invoices → Xero; enquiry → quote → follow-up; new client → onboarding pack) → managed service explained → pricing → CTA.

**CTA framing:** "Bring your spreadsheet to a 20-minute call. We'll tell you exactly what it could do on its own."

## V7 — What Does Your Admin Cost?

**Hook:** "What does your admin actually cost you?"
**Subhead:** Five hours a week at your billable rate is £6,000+ a year — before the late invoices and missed follow-ups. We find the leak, then plug it.

**Structure:** Cost math hero → leakage breakdown → the audit-style offer → pricing → CTA.

**CTA framing:** "20 minutes to find your number."

---

## V8 — The Anti-Agency

**Hook:** "No dashboards. No 'AI transformation'. Your admin, done."
**Subhead:** We're not an agency selling you a platform. We build one workflow that works, run it for you, and only then build the next one.

**Structure:** What we're not (agency clichés, struck through) → what we do (one workflow at a time) → guardrails (you keep the approve button; your accountant's process untouched; drafts, never auto-sends; cancel monthly) → pricing → CTA.

**CTA framing:** "3 founding clients. One workflow each. £200/month. Cancel if it doesn't pay for itself."
