## Offer ladder

### Free front door

**15-minute Clinic Leakage Review**

A quick call to identify where a clinic loses enquiries between phone, WhatsApp, Instagram, website forms and booking links.

### Paid diagnostic

**72-hour Clinic Leakage Audit — £500**

Deliverables:

- enquiry journey map
- leakage scorecard
- top 3 revenue/admin leaks
- automation blueprint
- first workflow recommendation
- implementation quote

The £500 is credited against the build.

### Implementation sprint

**Clinic Automation Sprint — from £2,500**

Build one workflow end-to-end:

- missed enquiry callback
- WhatsApp/DM follow-up
- no-show reduction
- post-treatment aftercare + reviews
- old-lead reactivation

## Daily plan — first 14 days

### Day 1 — Funnel + list foundation

- Publish the Clinic Leakage Review funnel.
- Create first 25-clinic intelligence list.
- Pick top 10 P1 clinics.
- Prepare personalised first-line observations for each.

Success metric: funnel live locally, 10 clinics ready to contact.

### Day 2 — First outreach batch

- Send 10 personalised emails/DMs.
- Call the top 5 after the message.
- Use opener: “Quick question — do you handle new treatment enquiries in-house, or does that go through a central booking team?”
- Log every outcome.

Success metric: 10 contacted, 5 calls attempted, 1 reply or warm signal.

### Day 3 — Mini-audits

- Create 3 one-page mini-audits for the best-fit clinics.
- Focus each audit on visible leakage: slow form route, unclear WhatsApp, weak booking CTA, no review flow.
- Send as follow-up.

Success metric: 3 concrete clinic-specific assets sent.

### Day 4 — Voice AI demo

- Build or configure a demo missed-enquiry callback agent.
- Output fields: treatment interest, timing, booking status, objection, summary.
- Record/demo internally before showing prospects.

Success metric: demo ready enough to show on a call.

### Day 5 — Follow-up + call day

- Follow up the first 10.
- Contact 10 new clinics.
- Call 10 total.
- Push for the free 15-minute review, not the £500 audit yet.

Success metric: 1 review booked.

### Day 6 — Authority asset

- Publish the “7-Point Aesthetic Clinic Leakage Checklist”.
- Add it to the funnel as the proof/lead magnet.
- Use it in outreach.

Success metric: one useful asset prospects can receive.

### Day 7 — Review and adjust

- Review open/reply/call outcomes.
- Keep the best subject line and opener.
- Cut weak clinic segments.
- Decide whether to scale London Harley/Chelsea or add Manchester/Birmingham.

Success metric: clear go/no-go on the current wedge.

### Day 8 — Scale to 30 more clinics

- Add 30 clinics to the intelligence list.
- Prioritise aesthetic clinics with WhatsApp + booking + forms.
- Contact 15.

Success metric: 40–50 total clinics in pipeline.

### Day 9 — Convert review to paid audit

- Run any booked free reviews.
- Close with: “I can map this properly in a 72-hour audit. It’s £500 and comes off the implementation if you build.”
- Send invoice/payment link if agreed.

Success metric: first £500 audit agreed or clear objection captured.

### Day 10 — Build proof from objections

- Turn objections into FAQ and landing-page copy.
- Add “We work around your booking system — we do not replace it.”
- Add “AI stays behind the scenes; your client experience remains premium.”

Success metric: funnel improved from real conversations.

### Day 11 — Second outreach batch

- Contact 20 more clinics.
- Use tighter copy based on Day 7/10 learnings.
- Call top 10.

Success metric: 2–3 review calls in calendar.

### Day 12 — Paid audit delivery template

- Build the audit report template.
- Sections: current journey, leakage points, quick wins, workflow spec, implementation quote.
- Prepare before first paid client lands.

Success metric: paid work can be delivered fast.

### Day 13 — Close implementation

- For any audit/review with clear pain, propose one £2,500 sprint.
- Keep scope narrow: one workflow only.
- Require deposit before build.

Success metric: first implementation proposal sent.

### Day 14 — Pipeline review

- Calculate: contacted, replied, calls, reviews, audits, proposals, wins.
- If the wedge works: scale to 100 clinics.
- If not: adjust to one sharper workflow: missed-call recovery, no-show reduction, or post-treatment reviews.

Success metric: route to first £500/£2,500 is visible.
