## Core idea

A high-ticket AI service does not need to start as a full done-for-you build. It can start as a **done-with-you AI concierge**:

- mandatory pre-call intake,
- two focused strategy/build calls per month,
- async support with a clear response SLA,
- shared documentation hub,
- quantified list of every workflow/skill built,
- post-call follow-up automated with AI.

The client pays for access, guidance, and momentum — not just deliverables.

## Offer from the video

The video describes an AI concierge offer priced around **$1,000–$2,000/month**.

Components:

1. Pre-call intake form.
2. Two 45-minute done-with-you strategy calls per month.
3. Async access via Voxer with a 12-hour response SLA.
4. Shared Notion hub for recordings, notes, action items, and assets built.
5. Claude skills that automate call logs and follow-up emails.
6. Pricing ladder: start at $1,000, raise to $1,500, then $2,000/month when close rates remain high.
7. Cap client load to protect quality.

## Framework: AOA

The operating framework is:

1. **Audit** — find time sinks, repeated tasks, broken handoffs, and AI opportunities.
2. **Optimize** — improve the process before automating.
3. **Automate** — only hand the process to AI after it is clear.

Important principle:

> Automating a broken workflow just breaks things faster.

## Why this fits clinics

Clinics may not know exactly what automation to buy. A concierge offer lets them pay for ongoing guided improvement.

It reduces friction because the promise is not:

> Buy a big AI system.

It is:

> Every month, we remove one admin/enquiry bottleneck from your clinic.

This fits the aesthetic clinic reality:

- busy team,
- fragmented enquiries,
- no dedicated automation owner,
- high value per consultation,
- premium client experience matters,
- staff cannot spend weeks specifying workflows.

## Required assets to build

### 1. Clinic intake form

Questions:

- What are your top 3 recurring admin tasks?
- Which enquiry channel causes the most stress?
- What gets forgotten when the clinic is busy?
- What do clients ask repeatedly?
- What do staff chase manually?
- What systems do you use? Booking, CRM, WhatsApp, forms, email.
- What would a successful first month look like?

### 2. Notion clinic automation hub

Sections:

- clinic profile,
- systems map,
- enquiry channels,
- workflow backlog,
- call log,
- action items,
- workflows built,
- SOPs/scripts,
- monthly wins,
- next review.

### 3. Post-call automation

After each call:

- transcribe notes/recording,
- create call log,
- extract decisions,
- extract action items,
- draft client follow-up email,
- update workflow backlog,
- update “value delivered” list.

### 4. Monthly value ledger

This is important for retention.

Each month, show:

- workflows reviewed,
- automations built,
- staff time saved estimate,
- enquiry leaks reduced,
- scripts/templates created,
- next opportunities.
