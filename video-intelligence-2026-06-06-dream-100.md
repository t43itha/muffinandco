## Core idea

Do not wait for an audience. Get first customers by borrowing trust from people and places that already have your audience.

For Muffin & Co, that means aesthetic clinics are not the only target. The leverage targets are the people, communities, and channels that already talk to clinic owners, clinic managers, aesthetic practitioners, dermatology operators, medspa founders, and beauty business owners.

## Key frameworks from the video

### 1. Dream 100

Make a list of 100 people, organisations, communities, and channels that already reach your target customer.

For Muffin & Co, this list should include:

- aesthetic clinic consultants
- aesthetic training academies
- clinic growth marketers
- booking-system vendors
- clinic software vendors
- insurance/compliance providers
- aesthetics podcasts
- aesthetics newsletters
- Harley Street / London clinic directories
- local beauty/aesthetics business communities
- CPD/course providers
- private Facebook groups and WhatsApp communities for clinic owners
- medspa/aesthetic conference organisers
- finance/payment providers for clinics
- review/reputation vendors

### 2. Canvas strategy

Do not ask first. Give value first.

For Muffin & Co, that means each Dream 100 outreach should include one of:

- a tiny clinic leakage benchmark
- a free teardown of one public clinic enquiry journey
- a useful “missed enquiry recovery” checklist
- a co-branded free training idea
- a mini report: “Where aesthetic clinics lose consultations before booking”
- a ready-to-send newsletter blurb for their audience

### 3. Go where the audience lives

Do not rely on social posting from zero.

For Muffin & Co, we should join or monitor:

- aesthetic practitioner communities
- clinic owner Facebook groups
- Reddit/Quora threads around clinic marketing, booking systems, no-shows, and aesthetics admin
- industry webinars and comment sections
- software/vendor communities where clinic operators ask operations questions

Rule: be visibly useful before pitching.

### 4. Strategic gifting

Small, personal gestures can unlock relationships.

For Muffin & Co, gifts should be practical, not gimmicky:

- personalised one-page clinic leakage scorecard
- Loom audit of a clinic booking flow
- short benchmark comparing their booking flow to three local competitors
- “before/after” enquiry follow-up workflow map
- a polished PDF checklist they can use internally

### 5. Content only if it creates direct value

If we create content, it should not be generic AI content.

Useful Muffin & Co content:

- “I tested 10 London aesthetic clinic enquiry flows. Here is where leads leak.”
- “How fast do Harley Street clinics reply to new treatment enquiries?”
- “The missed-call script that recovers consultation bookings.”
- “Aesthetic clinic WhatsApp follow-up: 7-message template.”
- “Clinic no-show recovery workflow map.”

## Strategic implication for Muffin & Co

The funnel is only one asset. The distribution engine should be a Dream 100 relationship system.

Instead of only cold-emailing clinics, we build two lists:

1. **Clinic prospect list** — direct buyers.
2. **Dream 100 leverage list** — people who already reach many buyers.

This turns the £100 → £20k challenge from one-by-one cold outreach into relationship-led distribution.
